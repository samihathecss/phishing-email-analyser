    What to do next

      This is an educational triage tool, not a verdict. A “High Risk” score means many warning signs were present — not proof of an attack. A “Low Risk” score means few known signs were found — not proof of safety. Never test a suspicious email by clicking its links or opening its attachments.

      How this tool works (and how it doesn't)

      Runs on your device

      The analysis code runs inside your browser. There is no server, no database and no analytics. Your pasted email never leaves this page — you can even disconnect from the internet and it still works.

      Fully explainable

      Every warning sign is a named rule with a fixed point value. The results show exactly which rules fired, what text triggered them, and how the total was calculated — no mysterious number.

      Educational triage, not a verdict

      This tool counts how many common phishing warning signs an email contains. It cannot and does not decide whether an email is definitely malicious or definitely safe.

      Stay safe while you learn

      • Never click links or open attachments in a suspicious email just to see what happens.
      • To check whether a message is genuine, go directly to the organisation's official website, official app, or a phone number you already trust — never the contact details inside the suspicious email.
      • If the email reached a workplace account, report it to your IT or security team.
      • No automated check — including this one — can guarantee an email is safe. When in doubt, don't act; verify first.
